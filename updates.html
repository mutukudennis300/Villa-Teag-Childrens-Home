Stories and updates from Villa Teag

We share news about activities, learning, wellbeing, community support, and milestones at Villa Teag Children's Home with honesty and respect for every child's privacy. We recognise the people and partners who contribute to the home's work.

Our commitment to responsible storytelling

We publish updates only after the relevant information has been checked and approved. We do not publish a child's private history, sensitive case details, or identifiable photographs without the required authorisation and consent.

Education & learning

Celebrating learning

Our education updates celebrate school activities, learning milestones, and the support that helps children continue learning.

Children's privacy comes first.

Care & wellbeing

Everyday care matters

Our care updates highlight wellbeing, recreation, health awareness, and the routines that help children learn and grow in a supportive environment.

Children's dignity and privacy come first.

Community & partnerships

Working together

We recognise contributions from individuals, organisations, and community partners, and describe their support accurately and respectfully.

We publish acknowledgements with permission.
