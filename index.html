OUR FOUNDATION

Villa Teag Children’s Home is rooted in a commitment to care for children who need a safe and supportive place to grow. Our work centres on dignity, protection, learning and helping each child build skills for the future.

Through daily care, education and encouragement, our focus is to support children’s wellbeing and create opportunities for them to develop confidence, independence and hope through local educational opportunities and psychosocial care.

Core Pillars of Care

Villa Teag provides a stable and supportive environment for children. Through residential care, healthcare, counselling, mentorship, and education, we help children build safer, healthier and more independent futures.
