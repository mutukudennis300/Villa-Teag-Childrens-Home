Programmes and Services

Comprehensive care addressing every aspect of a child's development and long-term self-reliance.

Our Core Programmes

Our programmes put children's safety, individual needs, development, and future opportunities first. Each child receives services and care arrangements tailored to their circumstances and authorised care plan.

Education Programme

Structured academic placement and learning support tailored to each child's needs, including learning materials, school participation, and mentorship.

  • School placement and coordination with education providers
  • Individual tutoring and focused study groups
  • School uniforms and essential learning resources
  • Support with transitions between school levels
  • Secondary-school and vocational guidance pathways

Residential Care & Protection

A protective, caring living environment that supports children's day-to-day welfare, dignity and stability.

  • Safe accommodation and appropriate sleeping arrangements
  • Nutritious meals and structured meal routines
  • Hygiene supplies and personal-care guidance
  • Day-to-day welfare support and appropriate record keeping
  • Child safeguarding procedures and safety monitoring

Counselling & Psychosocial Support

Emotional and psychosocial support helps children develop resilience, healthy relationships and coping skills. We arrange specialist care when needed through appropriate providers.

  • Supportive individual conversations and referrals
  • Group discussions and positive mentorship
  • Care that is sensitive to difficult experiences
  • Healthy coping skills and emotional development
  • Respect for children's privacy and confidentiality

Recreation & Creative Activities

Age-appropriate recreational activities that support physical wellbeing, creativity, social connection and confidence in a safe setting.

  • Sports, movement and physical activities
  • Arts, music, dance and drama
  • Supervised outdoor play and outings
  • Hobby development and group clubs
  • Teamwork, confidence and positive peer relationships

Family Tracing & Reintegration

Family tracing and reintegration planning help children maintain or rebuild safe family connections through authorised, individual plans guided by each child's best interests.

  • Family tracing through appropriate authorised processes
  • Assessment of safety and family circumstances by relevant personnel
  • Planning for authorised family contact and visits
  • Carefully planned transitions based on the child's best interests
  • Follow-up and referrals after a placement change

Life Skills & Youth Development

Practical learning and mentorship to prepare older children and young people for greater responsibility, further education and independent living.

  • Vocational exploration and career guidance
  • Leadership, communication and character development
  • Financial literacy and budgeting basics
  • Cooking, hygiene and home-management skills
  • Transition planning and self-reliance workshops

Child Placement & Wellbeing Plan

Care planning addresses each child's safety, health, education, development, and long-term interests.
