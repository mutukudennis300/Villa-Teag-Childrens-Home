Get Involved

Make a meaningful difference. Explore ways to volunteer, support our work, or build a partnership with Villa Teag Children's Home.

Your support can make a difference

Whether you volunteer, propose a partnership, or contribute essential supplies, our team will help arrange a safe, useful way to support the home.

Ways to Get Involved

Service & Safeguarding

Your Heart, Their Development

Share your skills and time through a structured, safeguarding-focused volunteer enquiry. The team arranges every role and visit, and completes the required suitability checks and approvals.

Volunteer Opportunities

Contact the home to discuss appropriate roles, availability, supervision and safeguarding requirements.

Academic Tutor

By arrangement

Support learning through appropriate tutoring, study assistance and educational activities coordinated with the home's team.

Useful skills: Teaching and patience

Life Skills Mentor

By arrangement

Share practical knowledge and positive guidance to help young people build confidence and prepare for future responsibilities.

Useful skills: Mentoring and listening

Health and Hygiene Support

By arrangement

Qualified volunteers can support health education, hygiene awareness, and other approved activities.

Useful skills: Relevant health training

Skills Facilitator

By arrangement

Offer practical workshops in computing, crafts, career awareness or other skills agreed with the home's management.

Useful skills: A practical or professional skill

Activity Leader

By arrangement

Help facilitate approved sports, art, music or recreational activities that encourage creativity and healthy social interaction.

Useful skills: Creativity and teamwork

General Support

By arrangement

Ask about approved administrative, facilities, library organisation or other practical tasks that match the home's current needs.

Useful skills: Reliability and willingness to help
